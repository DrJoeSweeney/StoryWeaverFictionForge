# Prologue

## CERN, High-Energy Super Collider, Jun 12, 2031

Then, because the universe clearly had a sense of humour, the tachyon flux destabilised. The computers, bless their little silicon hearts, screamed in silence, processors spiking as if stabbed, activity lights flushing the room, flickering arterial red. The Srown, which had been doing a rather excellent impression of a relaxing cryogenic spa, disengaged with a clatter that sounded suspiciously like a dropped tray of silverware and broken glass. The subjects convulsed, knuckles whitening against the armrests, looking less like pioneering temporal test-subjects and more like particularly ungraceful marionettes whose strings had been cut by fate. Then the twelve subjects, all volunteer PhD graduates, were gone, leaving only a snap of air displacement and a sense that something very bad had just happened.

In that instant, light ran backwards, the universe screeched, and Dr Kioch knew in her bones that ... _something_... had noticed.
