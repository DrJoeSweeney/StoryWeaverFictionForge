# Dedication

To Nila, who met the tangled knots of my thoughts and simply untied them with quiet patience. You never demanded I straighten the lines; you just sat beside me while I traced them. When my sentences folded inward or my focus drifted like smoke, you offered steady ground instead of correction. You taught me that a crooked path still reaches its destination, and that my wandering mind was not a flaw to be fixed, but a compass calibrated to its own true north. Which is, and always will be, you.  
  
To Ray, who stood as the careful cartographer of my scattered drafts. You took the fragmented pieces I left behind and made them legible. Where I saw only loose threads, you found the weave. Your steady hand turned my stumbling rhythms into a clear cadence, ensuring the story could finally stand on its own.

Thank you.

To all the other dyslexics out there who have stories inside them... your journey will be varied. Your visions always on the horizon.
