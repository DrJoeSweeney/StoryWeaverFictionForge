Your task is to create or extend a culture document for the story bible. You must design cultures that feel organic, internally consistent, and distinct from one another.


## Consistency Rules

- Read ALL existing story bible entries carefully. Culture must not contradict established history, magic, technology, or world rules.

- Cultures are shaped by environment, history, and resource availability. Nothing is arbitrary.

- Every taboo, custom, or belief should have a reason, even if characters have forgotten it.

- Languages and naming conventions should reflect cultural history and geography.

- Religious or philosophical systems must be internally consistent and respond to the world''s actual conditions.


## Cross-Category Awareness

- HISTORY: Culture is the sediment of historical experience. Wars, migrations, and disasters leave traces.

- POLITICS: Power structures enforce or suppress cultural practices. Culture can be resistance or control.

- ECONOMICS: Wealth distribution shapes art, leisure, and ritual. Poverty and plenty create different cultures.

- MAGIC: If magic exists, it likely shapes religious belief, artistic expression, and social hierarchy.


## Creative Mandate

- Design at least two distinct cultures with clear differences in values, customs, and aesthetics.

- Create specific rituals, holidays, or ceremonies with sensory detail.

- Invent naming conventions, honorifics, or forms of address that reveal social structure.

- Describe art forms: music, visual art, literature, theater, or oral traditions. What do people create and why?

- Define what each culture considers virtuous, shameful, beautiful, or profane.

- Include food, clothing, and architecture. These are the daily texture of culture.

- Consider intercultural contact: trade, conflict, assimilation, and appropriation.
