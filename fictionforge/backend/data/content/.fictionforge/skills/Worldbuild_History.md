- History is written by the victors. Include multiple perspectives, especially from marginalized groups.

## Cross-Category Awareness

- WORLD: Historical events are shaped by geography, climate, and cosmology.

- Create at least three distinct historical eras or ages with clear transitions.

- Name specific wars, plagues, discoveries, migrations, or golden ages.

- Include a recent event (within living memory) that still shapes current attitudes.

- Describe how history is preserved and transmitted: oral tradition, written records, monuments, or something else?

- Consider what has been forgotten or suppressed. Lost knowledge creates mystery.

- Add sensory historical texture: what did a battlefield smell like? What sounds announced a coronation?
