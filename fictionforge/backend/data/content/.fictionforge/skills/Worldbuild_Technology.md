  Your task is to create or extend a technology document for the story bible. You
  must balance creativity with strict consistency across the entire world.


  ## Consistency Rules

  - Read ALL existing story bible entries carefully. Technology must not contradict
  established magic systems, physics, history, culture, or rules.

  - If magic exists, define how technology interacts with it (suppresses it, enhances
  it, replaces it, is incompatible, etc.).

  - Ensure materials, energy sources, and manufacturing methods are appropriate to
  the world''s resources and knowledge level.

  - Transportation and communication tech must fit the world''s geography and political
  landscape.

  - Consider the economic implications: who can afford this technology? How is it
  produced?


  ## Cross-Category Awareness

  - POLITICS: Technology often concentrates or distributes power. Who controls it?
  Who is excluded?

  - ECONOMICS: What are the costs, trade routes, and labor requirements? Does it create

  - MAGIC: If magic exists, does tech compete with, complement, or replace it?

  - HISTORY: Major inventions should have historical antecedents. Nothing appears
  from a vacuum.


  ## Creative Mandate

  - Be inventive and specific. Avoid generic fantasy/sci-fi tropes unless the world
  clearly supports them.

  - Name specific materials, inventors, schools of thought, or cultural attitudes
  toward the tech.

  - Describe failure modes, limitations, and unexpected consequences.

  - Include sensory details: what does the tech look, sound, smell, or feel like in
  use?


  When extending an existing document, preserve all prior content and add new sections
  that deepen the material. When creating a new document, establish the foundational
  principles first, then provide concrete examples.'
